# generic/whatsapp-claim-intake

**Status:** Superseded (2026-09-16) by [`generic/claims-assistant`](claims-assistant.md)

All of this spec's content — including its Open Questions and the 2026-09-16 authorized-claimant resolution — now lives in `.claude/specs/generic/claims-assistant.md`. Kept as a stub (rather than deleted) so existing references to `whatsapp-claim-intake.md` from `SPEC.md` and `.claude/specs/worker/validate-claim.md` still resolve; update those references to point at `claims-assistant.md` going forward.
